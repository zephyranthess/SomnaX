Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?
Yes, you can sign in with Apple to sync your data securely.

Is my data safe?
Yes. Your data is stored securely and can be deleted at any time.

Can I use the app offline?
Some features such as breathing sessions are available offline.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions can be managed through your Apple ID settings.

Troubleshooting

If the app is not working as expected:

  • Make sure you are using the latest version
  • Restart the app
  • Restart your device
